Service Description

Begin your design journey with our Initial Consultation Call. We offer a call to discuss your vision and answer any questions you may have. During this call, you will: - Share your design goals and preferences - Learn about our services and how we can help - Get a sense of our approach and value This initial call is a great way to explore how we can transform your space. Book your call today and take the first step towards your dream design.

Cancellation Policy

Our Complimentary Call Booking service is designed to provide you with an introductory discussion about your design needs and our services. Please review the following policy to ensure a smooth and productive call: Scheduling: Free calls are scheduled by appointment only. Appointments can be booked through our website or by contacting our office directly. Preparation: Clients are encouraged to prepare any relevant information or questions in advance to make the most of the call. Call Scope: The call includes an opportunity to share your design goals and preferences, learn about our services, and understand our approach. This service does not include detailed design advice or specific project estimates, which are part of our more comprehensive consultation services. Duration: The typical duration of the free call is 30 minutes. Cancellation and Rescheduling: Cancellations or rescheduling requests should be made at least 12 hours in advance to allow us to accommodate other clients. Confidentiality: All information shared during the call will be treated with the utmost confidentiality and used solely for the purpose of providing you with the best possible design service. By booking a Free Call with Decor Republic, you agree to the terms outlined in this policy. We look forward to discussing your design needs and helping you take the first step towards transforming your space.

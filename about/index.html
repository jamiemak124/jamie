About Decor Republic

About Decor Republic 

Decor Republic is Hong Kong’s trusted, innovative interior design studio specializing in bespoke residential and commercial spaces. We believe that every space should reflect your unique personality, lifestyle, and aspirations.

Contact Us

OUR VISION

We weaves stories into spaces, transforming ordinary rooms into extraordinary experiences. Imagine stepping into a space where every corner reflects your essence, every detail whispers your personality, and every design choice feels made just for you. 

Design isn’t just about aesthetics—it’s about creating a narrative. Your space tells a story, and we’re here to ensure it’s unforgettable. Let us guide you through a transformation journey, where your dreams and our expertise converge to create a masterpiece uniquely yours.

WHY US

Personalized Designs
Our designs incorporate your personal style, preferences, and routines, ensuring that every corner of your home or office feels uniquely yours.
 
Functionality Meets Aesthetics
Maximising small spaces, we blend beauty with functionality for both cozy apartments and expansive commercial areas.
 
Seamless Experience
From consultation to completion, enjoy a hassle-free journey with direct 1:1 contact with our designers. We value your feedback and ensure clear communication throughout the process, making revisions smooth and collaborative.
 
Quality Craftsmanship
We deliver high-quality results with locally sourced materials and expert craftsmanship, ensuring high-quality results at affordable rates.

OUR FOUNDER

 With years of experience in residential and commercial design, Jamie founded Decor Republic in 2020, which represents the perfect mix of creativity, skill, and passion.
 
She believes that interior design is all about open-mindedness and a willingness to embrace new ideas, and it is this approach that has helped her to build a broad and loyal client base. Whether you are looking to redesign your home or office, Jamie and her team have the design expertise you need to turn your vision into reality.
 
 She also believes that work-life balance is key to success. "Leisure" is one of the main words in her dictionary. She lived on the island of Bali for years, working with developers on villa designs, before moving back to Hong Kong.

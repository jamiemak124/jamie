Mount Pavilia

1200 square feet

Located in Clear Water Bay in Hong Kong, which has all the natural surroundings and a quiet environment.

The apartment itself has a large window in the living room with a stunning green mountain view.

The interior design is based on simple nature feelings and well using natural light to brighten up the whole space. The owner is a book lover, he needs a lot of storage for the books. With the size of the shelf and the LED light behind the bookshelf, the library wall becomes the focus of the house.

Projects / Commercial

121bc

1204 square feet

An Italian restaurant in Soho Hong Kong, right on the busy street of the nightlife area.

The design is some interior luxury elements matching their signature chandelier. With full height windows letting the sunlight is brighter space as well. In the progress of making the design, The owners decided to change the location instead of renewing the old one all of a sudden.

The new location is an underground site, it used to be a restaurant as well. Underground, Dark, and space a bit bigger…A cellar would be nice!

The design is from a luxury fancy restaurant to a Tuscan wine cellar. I used washed brick walls to enhance the vintage aged style, with dim lighting the whole space will give a sense of warmness and relaxation.

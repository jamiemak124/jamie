TOROK VILLA 694 square feet

Located in Lombok island, Indonesia. 

Private 3 Bedrooms villa located in Torok Lombok, Indonesia. It's a tropical paradise, it's a canvas with green and blue. For the interior, with all those natural colors from the large window, we choose to use natural wood material to match the environment surrounding it.

The most famous wood in Indonesia is Teak wood, most of the high-end furniture is made by this kind of wood. With stunning view and comfy furniture, who wouldn't want to just stay here forever?

La Vinoteca

500 square feet

The wine bar is located in Kuta Lombok, a paradise island.

It is an expansion of the office - Invest Islands, the owners love wine and they wanted to build the first wine bar in town. The interior matched the industrial style office, the use of wood and green makes a sense of harmony and also brightens the space.

The custom-made bar and wine racks also enhance the design. When you turn all the lighting on at night time, no one can not take a look and wonder what’s going on when passing by.

Fleuria Atelier

3000+ square feet

Fleuria Atelier is a stunning 3,000 sq. ft. floral studio located in Lai Chi Kok, designed to provide an exceptional experience for its customers. The space seamlessly combines functionality and elegance, featuring a back office area, workshop spaces, a chill room for flowers, and ample storage. With a bright and classic aesthetic, the studio creates a welcoming atmosphere that reflects its dedication to quality and style.

The lift lobby is adorned with the brand’s signature colors, enhanced by carefully crafted moldings and vintage wooden floors, adding a timeless charm to the space. Every detail has been thoughtfully curated to ensure that visitors enjoy a delightful and inspiring environment.

Whispers of Nature

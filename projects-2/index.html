Frequently Asked Questions

What interior design services do you offer?

We offer bespoke interior design-and-build services for homes and businesses in Hong Kong. This includes full-service interior design, space planning, home styling, custom furniture design, and design consultations. If you want a complete redesign or support with a specific part of your project, we can help you create a space that feels functional, stylish, and made for you.

What does your design process look like?

Our process is simple and flexible. We begin with an initial consultation to understand your vision, preferences, and budget. Next, we develop the concept, then share mood boards, 3D renderings, and material samples so you can review the design with confidence. Once you approve the plan, we handle procurement and oversee installation to keep everything smooth and hassle-free.

How do you help me discover my design style?

We start by getting to know you, your taste, and how you want to live or work in the space. Then we guide you with clear design advice and a tailored plan that fits your style and needs. You stay involved at every step, so the final result feels personal, practical, and true to you.

Can I see examples of your past projects?

Absolutely! We believe that our previous work speaks volumes about our capabilities and style. You can check out Our Projects on our website to see a variety of projects we have completed, ranging from modern apartments to luxurious office spaces. Each project is a testament to our commitment to quality and detail. If you need more specific examples, feel free to contact us, and we'll be happy to share more with you.

What is the typical timeline for a project?

The timeline for a project can vary depending on its scope and complexity. For smaller projects such as home styling or a single room redesign, it might take a few weeks. Larger projects, like a full apartment renovation or commercial space design, can take several months. During the initial consultation, we will provide a more accurate timeline based on your specific needs. We pride ourselves on our efficiency and strive to meet deadlines without compromising on quality. Book your free consultation now!

How much should I budget for my project?

Your budget depends on the size of your space, the scope of work, and the level of finish you want. We talk openly about your budget during the initial consultation and help you get the best value from it. Our pricing is transparent, with no hidden costs, and we offer both design-only and design-and-build options.

How do I start a project with Decor Republic?

Getting started is easy. Book your complimentary consultation, and we’ll talk through your space, style preferences, goals, and budget. This helps us understand what you need and how we can support you. After that, we prepare a proposal with the project scope and estimated costs, so you know what to expect from the start.

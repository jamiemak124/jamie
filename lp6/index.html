LP6

300 square feet

This apartment radiates classic French elegance, blending refined details with everyday comfort. Decorative wall paneling, gold-accented fixtures, and a statement chandelier evoke Parisian charm, while a rich emerald sofa and curated artwork add warmth and sophistication. The open-plan layout maximizes space, seamlessly connecting the inviting living area with a stylish kitchen featuring timeless cabinetry and a chic breakfast bar. Soft, neutral tones are paired with luxurious textures, creating a serene, hotel-like atmosphere. Every detail is thoughtfully chosen to reflect a sense of effortless grace, transforming this compact apartment into a truly elegant French-inspired retreat in the heart of the city.

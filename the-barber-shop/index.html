THE BARBER SHOP 650 square feet

The Barber Shop’s interior design is based on the Irish bar style. The shop is a barbershop in the daytime and a wine bar at night for private events. At the waiting area, we have a custom-made vintage leather Chesterfield sofa with a grandma-style floor light, you can totally relax here with a glass of red while you are waiting for a haircut.

Next to the waiting area is the bar area, the custom-made bar counter is the highlight of the barbershop. The first thing customers see when they step in from the entrance will be the bar, the curve at the corner made people feel welcome.

What I like the most is the molding details of the barber stations, although the wood panels are in the same color you can still see the details clearly. The tile flooring design is very interesting, we made the welcome sign at the front door.

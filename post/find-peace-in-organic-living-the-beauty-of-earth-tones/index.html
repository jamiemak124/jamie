Find Peace in Organic Living | The Beauty of Earth Tones

Jamie Mak · Oct 3, 2020 · 2 min read

Last Updated: October 03, 2020

 

The Earth Tones Trend

Bringing the outdoors in has always been a trend. For decades, homeowners have loved the natural and organic appeal of elements that make them feel serene, calm and, above all, welcomed into a space. For those looking for a soothing and glamorous environment that is both relaxed and pleasing to the eye, this color palette appears to impress and is a safe choice.

Invite Peace And Comfort Into Your Space


Earth tones bring a sense of Zen, which makes it so peaceful yet striking at the same time. You can rely on this vibe in building a warm, soothing home interior. Comfortable furniture and cozy, stacked rugs can be useful in creating a personal oasis within your home.


With more historical homes and furniture, the rich feeling of olive green or mustard will work well, helping to create a quiet sense of timelessness. If you are going for a clean and modern look for your space, try looking for simple shapes and sleek silhouettes with interesting angles. Minimalist furniture designs suit this style perfectly, creating a modern organic space.


Bringing Nature Into Your Home

Earth tones fit and balance together even better when you actually put nature into the room. The secret to organic modern design is hints of it. The freshness of the outside is brought in by real or fake plants, so it is always a good idea to have lots of plants in a room. Consider searching for more natural materials such as wood, leather , wool, and natural fibers for furniture and accessories as well. To add warmth, you can opt for wood grains. The earthy appeal can then be complemented by leafy designs, marble, and cowhide.

Get on board and wash away the tension of a long and busy day by using colors pulled from nature to create a harmonious environment right in your home!

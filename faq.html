A few helpful answers

Good to know.

A few things people often ask before starting a design project.

What interior design services do you offer?

We create bespoke interiors for homes and businesses in Hong Kong. Depending on your needs, our work can include consultation, space planning, interior design, styling, custom furniture and design-and-build project coordination.

What does the design process look like?

We begin with a conversation about your space and goals, then develop a design direction with layouts, visual references and material ideas. Once the direction is agreed, we prepare details and drawings, coordinate the work and help with sourcing and installation according to the agreed scope.

What if I am not sure about my style?

That is completely fine. We learn about your taste, routines and priorities through conversation and references, then help shape a direction that feels right for you.

Can I see examples of past projects?

Yes. Visit the projects page to explore a selection of our residential and styling work.

How long does a project take?

Timing depends on the size, scope and construction involved. Smaller projects may take a few weeks, while larger renovations can take several months. We discuss a realistic schedule during the consultation.

How do you work with budgets?

Budget depends on the space, scope and finish level. We discuss priorities early and outline the agreed scope and costs before work begins. Design-only and broader project support can be discussed.

How do I get started?

Contact us to arrange an initial conversation. We will learn about your project and outline suitable next steps.

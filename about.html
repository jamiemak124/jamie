The studio · Est. 2020

Spaces shaped
by your story.

Decor Republic is an independent Hong Kong interior design studio. We believe the best spaces feel personal, work beautifully and make everyday life better.

Our vision.

Weaving stories into spaces is at the heart of what we do. From a home that welcomes you back at the end of the day to a workplace that helps a team thrive, each environment should reflect the people who use it.

Why work with us?

We bring a personal approach, careful planning and quality craftsmanship to every project. Design decisions balance beauty and function, and you stay connected to the process with direct communication and clear milestones.

PERSONAL BY DESIGN

We listen first.

Your routines, preferences and priorities shape the direction from day one.

THOUGHT THROUGH

Beauty with purpose.

Layouts, materials and lighting are selected to make a space work as well as it looks.

CLEAR COMMUNICATION

A considered process.

We guide decisions and keep the project moving with clarity at each stage.

QUALITY CRAFT

Details matter.

We care about the finishing touches, the materials and the people who make them.

Our founder

Jamie Mak.

Jamie founded Decor Republic in 2020, bringing together a love of design, travel and the way places make people feel. Time spent in Bali broadened her appreciation for natural materials, relaxed living and craft. Today she brings that perspective to each Hong Kong home and business, creating spaces that feel individual, considered and enduring.

How we work

A clear path, together.

We listen to understand your needs, develop the design with you, document the details, coordinate the build, then source and style the finishing pieces. The scope is tailored to each project, so you can choose the level of support that suits you.
